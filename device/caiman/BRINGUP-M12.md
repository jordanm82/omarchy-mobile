# Pixel 9 Pro (caiman): Stage 12 — Kepler GNSS & Samsung S5400 Modem

Goal: enable the Pixel 9 Pro (`caiman`, Slot B) Samsung `s5400` cellular modem and Kepler GNSS engine through mainline `spmi_bit_bang`, `s5910`, `cp_pmic`, `pcie-gs201-cp`, `cpif`, `gnss_spi`, and `gnssif`, running vendor `rfsd`/`cbd`/`gnssd` (`modem` profile) and `android.hardware.gnss-service` + `omamobile-gnss-client` (`gnss-slsi` profile) strictly on RAM copies of NV partitions, while keeping Slot A untouched stock Android (`CP3A.261005.005`).

Stage 11 is complete ([BRINGUP-M11.md](BRINGUP-M11.md)). Path abbreviations follow [NOTES.md](NOTES.md): `R/` = phone hardware runs, `K/` = `linux-omarchy-mobile` (`7.3.0-rc6-omamobile-push1-g82e5e1d2b6ce-dirty`, commits `1cc43bea9`..`741a82088` on `omamobile-zumapro-caiman-modem`).

## 1. Kernel Changes, Root-Complex Fixes & Userspace Packaging

1. **Kernel (`K/`, `1cc43bea9`..`741a82088` on `omamobile-zumapro-caiman-modem`, `kernel/configs/omamobile.config`).**
   - **`spmi_bit_bang` (`1cc43bea9`) & `s5910` (`c164a6a4d`)**: added GPIO bit-bang SPMI controller driver and Samsung `s5910` clock-buffer SPMI driver for `sw_spmi_pmic` / `s5910` before PCIe link training.
   - **PCIe Root Complex (`70404aa2f`, `drivers/pci/controller/dwc/pcie-gs201-cp.c`)**:
     - Prepare `1_LANES` when `speed == 1` (`2_LANES` at `speed > 1`) in `cp_zuma_link_prepare` for initial Gen1 x1 bring-up.
     - Expand `CP_ATU_MEM_SIZE` from `SZ_2M` (`2 MiB`) to `SZ_8M` (`8 MiB`), because S5400 advertises four `1 MiB` BARs (`BAR2`–`BAR5`) ahead of 64-bit `BAR0` (`0x40400000`, `+4 MiB` offset), avoiding outbound iATU `UnsupReq` / `HeaderLog 40400000`.
     - Apply Google's `PCI_MSI_FLAGS_QMASK` (`0x4 << 1`, faking 16 MSI vectors) quirk in `cp_cfg_read` (cached per-EP as `u8 ep_msi_cap`, cleared in `cp_ep_forget`), matching stock `pcie-exynos-rc.c` because `0001:01:00.0` advertises `MSI: Count=1/1` while `cpif` allocates 4 MSI vectors.
   - **CPIF S5400 & CP PMIC (`f4da35ab5`, `drivers/soc/google/cpif/`)**: ported `S5400` support into `modem_prj.h`, `modem_v1.h`, `modem_ctrl.h`, `shm_ipc.c`, `modem_main.c`, `bootdump_io_device.c`, `modem_ctrl_s5100.c` (`power_reset_partial_cp`, `power_reset_warm_cp`, `pmic_warm_reset_sequence`, `check_cp_status_s5400`, `start_{normal,dump}_boot_*`, `"samsung,s5400-modem"`), `s51xx_pcie.c` (`MODEM_SEC_5400`, `0x5400`), and added `cp_pmic.c`/`.h`.
   - **Device tree & overlay (`741a82088`, [modem-cpif.dtso](overlays/modem-cpif.dtso))**: wired `pcie_2`, `sw_spmi_pmic`, `s5910`, `s2mpg16` `cp_pmic`, far-alive bias/float states (`cp2ap_float`, `cp2ap_wakeup_up`), modem GPIOs, `gnss_spi`, and `gnss_ipc` in `zumapro-caiman.dts`; enabled `DTB_OVERLAYS="aoc-sound modem-cpif"` in [device.conf](device.conf) and `packages/omamobile-device-caiman/device.conf`.
   - **`tools/kernel-pr-check`** (`82e5e1d2b..741a82088` in `omamobile-build:24.04` with `clang-r596125`): passed `no Co-Authored-By trailers`, `subjects have subsystem prefixes`, `checkpatch --strict: no errors` (`69` warnings/checks), `policy (tools/pr-check): identifiers, blobs`, `W=1 builds of 9 touched C files`, and `11 device trees build with W=1, no new warnings` (expected `not ok - sign-offs` prior to human `Signed-off-by`; `dtbs_check` skipped without `dtschema`).
