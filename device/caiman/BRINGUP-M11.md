# Pixel 9 Pro (caiman): Stage 11 — Ultrasonic Fingerprint (`qfs4008` / `qbt_handler`)

Goal: enable the Pixel 9 Pro (`caiman`, Slot B) Qualcomm `qfs4008` ultrasonic under-display fingerprint sensor through mainline `qbt_handler`, Trusty (`qfs.app` + `D1=A` `td.img`/`tdp.img` loop stores via `storageproxyd`), `qfp-daemon`, `omamobile-fp-bridge` (`--lhbm none`), `libfprint`, and `fprintd` across two boots, while keeping Slot A untouched stock Android (`CP3A.261005.005`).

Stage 10 is complete ([BRINGUP-M10.md](BRINGUP-M10.md)). Path abbreviations follow [NOTES.md](NOTES.md): `R/` = phone hardware runs, `K/` = `linux-omarchy-mobile` (`7.3.0-rc6-omamobile-push1-ge8c5cacde030`, committed as `82e5e1d2b` on `omamobile-zumapro-caiman-fp`).

## 1. Root Cause, Kernel Fixes & Package Changes

1. **Kernel (`K/`, commit `82e5e1d2b` on `omamobile-zumapro-caiman-fp`, `CONFIG_QBT_HANDLER=m` in `kernel/configs/omamobile.config`).**
   - **PMIC rail (`s2mpg15.h`, `sec-acpm.c`, `s2mps11.c`)**: exposed `S2MPG15_PMIC_L22S_CTRL` (`0x44`, voltage group 6: `700000` uV base, `25000` uV step, `64` levels, `BIT(7)` enable) as `ldo22s` (`.id = 21`).
   - **Mainline `qbt_handler` driver & DT binding (`drivers/misc/qbt_handler.c`, `Documentation/devicetree/bindings/misc/qcom,qbt-handler.yaml`)**: ported Google's GPL `qfs4008` IPC/FD misc driver to `gpiod_*`, `devm_regulator_get_enable_optional(..., "vdd")`, `class_create("qbt")`, `DEFINE_SIMPLE_DEV_PM_OPS`, and guarded `!is_wuhb_connected` around `disable_irq(0)` / wake handling so `/dev/qbt_fd` and `/dev/qbt_ipc` stay clean when `wuhb` is absent.
   - **Device tree (`zumapro-caiman.dts`)**: wired `qcomfp_intr1` (`gpa7-6`, `EXYNOS_PIN_FUNC_EINT`, `EXYNOS_PIN_PULL_UP`, `drv=<0>`) under `&pinctrl_alive`, enabled `ldo22s` (`1800000`–`1900000` uV, `regulator-always-on`), and added root node `fingerprint` (`compatible = "qcom,qbt-handler"`, `ipc-gpios = <&gpa7 6 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>`, `vdd-supply = <&ldo22s>`).
   - **`tools/kernel-pr-check`** (`32264e98e..82e5e1d2b` with `clang-r596125`): passed `no Co-Authored-By trailers`, `subjects have subsystem prefixes`, `checkpatch --strict: no errors` (`9` warnings/checks), `policy (tools/pr-check): identifiers, blobs`, `W=1 builds of 3 touched C files`, and `11 device trees build with W=1, no new warnings` (expected failure on `sign-offs` before human `Signed-off-by`; `dt_binding_check`/`dtbs_check` skipped without `dtschema` in container).
   - **Pre-boot `F6a`/`F6b` hardware checks** `[V]` (`R/`): `regmap 44: ac` (`vdd_udfps 1800mV`, `enable=1`), `qbt_handler` blacklisted at boot (`/dev/qbt*` absent until probed), `/dev/qbt_fd` (`501, 0`), `/dev/qbt_ipc` (`501, 1`), IRQ `gpa7 6 Edge qbt_ipc`, `QBT_IS_WUHB_CONNECTED: 0`, `QBT_TOUCH_FD_VERSION: 3`, and `QBT_ENABLE_IPC`/`QBT_DISABLE_IPC` ioctls verified without disrupting `synaptics-tcm spi0.0`.
2. **AIDL touch routing fix (`props-caiman.conf`) & userspace (`omamobile-fingerprint` `0.1.19-1`, `omamobile-device-caiman` `0.10.0-1`, `libfprint` `1.94.100-1.7`).**
   - Disassembly of `libqfp-service.so` (`QfpTz::openSensor()`, `QfpTz::readEvent()`, `QfpTz::reportFingerEvent()`) showed `qfp-daemon` defaults `persist.vendor.qfp.fd_events_src` to `0`, which polls `/dev/qbt_fd` and drops all AIDL `onPointerDownWithContext` / `onPointerUpWithContext` events from `omamobile-fp-bridge`. Setting `persist.vendor.qfp.fd_events_src=1` in [props-caiman.conf](../../packages/omamobile-fingerprint/lib/props-caiman.conf) (alongside `persist.vendor.qfp.enable_pv=12 0 0`) instructs `QfpTz` to open an internal `eventfd` and deliver `omamobile-fp-bridge` pointer touches directly to `qfs.app`.
   - Extended [tools/extract-fingerprint-vendor](../../tools/extract-fingerprint-vendor) (`-d caiman`: `137 files, 34M` from `CP3A.261005.005`), updated [omamobile-fp-bridge.c](../../fingerprint/bridge/omamobile-fp-bridge.c) to skip LHBM sysfs ops when `--lhbm none`, added `caiman` support in `omamobile-fp-chroot` (`TA=qfs.app`, `TA_PROBE=qfs`, `FP_HAL=/vendor/bin/hw/qfp-daemon`, `LHBM_DEV=none`, `PANEL=1280x2856`, `MAP_STORES=1`, `/dev/qbt_{fd,ipc}`, `/mnt/vendor/persist/qti_fp` & `/data/vendor/misc/qti_fp`), added `omamobile-caiman-fingerprint.conf` (`blacklist sg`, `blacklist qbt_handler`) in `omamobile-device-caiman` `0.10.0-1`, and packaged `omamobile-fingerprint` `0.1.19-1` + `libfprint` `1.94.100-1.7` + Arch `fprintd` `1.94.5-2`.
   - **Package tests** `[V]`: `packages/omamobile-fingerprint/test/{all,preflight}` and `packages/omamobile-device-caiman/test/all` passed all assertions inside `omamobile-alarm-build` (`1` skipped container-ns check: `alive() in a PID namespace # SKIP no unprivileged user namespaces here`).
